One should at this point recognize that activities are maintained in a data structure called the **Back Stack**.

This means that newly created activities are pushed onto this **stack**(LIFO).

Also, if the same activity is pushed on multiple times the stack fills up!!

This might not be our intention. So, control is needed

Popping from the stack is done by the finish command.
