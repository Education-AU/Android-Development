<div class="center-left">

1. Activities typically inherit from ComponentActivity and has overridden `OnCreate()` at least
2. All activities must be in the manifest but for simple activities no intent-filter is necessary
3. Navigation to other activities is done by intents and start activity where the Intent so far is a simple **from-to**
   piece of data.
4. Popping an activity is done by finish
