We are going to explore the life cycle of activities in a simple two activities application

The *launched* activity is The Pi-Activity, 

which is a simple activity that displays some text and has a navigation button for navigating to Tiger-Activity

The tiger Activity can finish and navigate back to the Pi-Activity


We need the manifest to declare the activities, and we need to set the Pi-Activity as the launcher activity
