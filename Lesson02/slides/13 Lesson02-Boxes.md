One can notice:
1. The size is **not** overridden by the modifier.
2. The color **is** overridden by the modifier.
3. Both borders are added.


It is not absolutely clear what the rules of overriding are but

1. Size type modifiers are generally not overridden the first size has mandate
2. Color type modifier are overridden
3. Borders are added
4. Paddings are added
5. And many more rather specialized rules
